# Walkthrough (spoilers)

The train always stands on the east-west track with the locomotive at the east end; the compass
at the top of the screen shows the direction. After each chapter: study the route map in the
cab, press **Set course**, make sure everyone is aboard and pull the control lever.

## Chapter I - Blackmoor Siding

1. Read the dispatcher's note in the passenger car (required).
2. Take a flashlight and the fuel can from the cargo car (rear). The toolbox in the workshop
   car holds a hammer and bolts.
3. Put the fuel can into the firebox in the cab (required).
4. Optional: the dispatcher's hut south of the passenger car and the crate in the yard hold supplies.

## Chapter II - Ravenwood Station

The signal ahead is red and dead. The train will not pass it.

1. Station building, office on the east side of the hall: the desk holds the **warehouse key**.
   The telegram on the ticket counter is a document.
2. Warehouse (west end of the platform, marked GOODS): two fuel cans, a fuse, a wrench, crates.
   Keep the wrench for the next chapter.
3. Signal box (east end): fuel the generator (1 fuel can), fit a fuse in the panel on the back
   wall (1 fuse), then pull the signal lever (required). The locker holds pliers and the
   **records room key**.
4. Optional: the records room behind the office holds the station master's log.

The Signal manifests as a red lamp with a hum. It jams flashlights and the compass and burns
at close range: move away from it.

## Chapter III - The Dead Forest

The railway switch ahead of the locomotive has been dismantled.

1. A derailed handcar lies beside the train on the north side (a pale lamp marks it): take the **crowbar**.
2. Trackwalker's hut south of the track near the switch: force the door with the crowbar. Inside:
   the **switch rod** and the trackwalker's note.
3. Follow the red rag flags north from the handcar to the search party's camp: the **switch
   lever**, the diary, a wrench in the crate, fuel in the barrel.
4. Rebuild the switch (lever + rod, with a wrench in the inventory) (required).
5. Optional: six containers in the forest count toward "Gather spare materials" (three needed).

The Hollow backs away from a flashlight beam and hunts by sound. Walk, do not sprint, and keep it lit.

## Chapter IV - The Empty Village

A connecting rod fails on arrival: engine condition drops to 22%. It must reach 60% (required).

1. Workshop (SMITH & WHEELWRIGHT) south of the lane: two sets of mechanical components, a
   wrench, scrap. A third set is in the second house from the west.
2. At the train's workbench craft an **engine part** (2 components + 1 scrap, wrench) and use
   it on the engine in the cab: +40%. Repair kits (+25%) and spare parts (+15%) also work.
3. Optional: the shop (PROVISIONS) has a **radio receiver**. Use it from the inventory while in
   the village: a voice reads four digits. Enter them on the lock of Voss's cellar east of the
   workshop. Inside: a ready engine part, a first aid kit and the inspector's confession.

The Conductor patrols the lane, investigates noise and chases on sight. Stay out of his view.

## Chapter V - The Last Tunnel

1. Works office (WORKS DEPT): the works order, a fuse and a crowbar in the locker, a fuel can.
2. Generator shed: start the portal generator (1 fuse + 1 fuel can) (required).
3. Gate parts: the **valve wheel** is in the STORES shed north of the track; the **gate gear**
   is in the crate by the tipped wagon far to the west.
4. Fit both at the winch on the south side of the portal and raise the gate (required).
5. Optional but decisive: force the watchman's hut south of the track near the portal with a
   crowbar and read the passenger manifest of train 13.
6. Walk into the tunnel. The junction lever is on the south wall. It starts set for the SERVICE BYPASS.
7. Return to the train and depart.

## Train reference

| Device | Accepts | Effect |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Firebox | fuel can | +26% fuel |
| Engine | engine part / repair kit / spare part | +40 / +25 / +15% |
| Fuse box | fuse / battery / wires | +40 / +20 / +10% electrics |
| Running gear | spare part / repair kit / bolts | +35 / +25 / +8% |
| Steam valve | - | -30 temperature |

Mark repairs 50% more per item. With no fuel cans at all, the firebox can be scraped for a
little fuel at the cost of engine condition, so a run cannot become unwinnable.
